    Title history
    
    
      
    
      Every title fight is saved once, with both fighters' scores, the rounds, the result and the rules version, so later score updates never change it. The top 3 contenders by Epoch Capabilities Index can challenge. A newcomer with no index yet can earn a shot by beating the #3 contender on the five rounds (a check, not a fight on record), and keeps its place in line until it gets it. First in line: newcomers in the top 3, by index; then newcomers that beat #3, by arrival on our roster; then the other top 3. Two models need results in at least 3 of the same rounds to fight, and never meet twice on the same scores. A draw keeps the belt. If nobody can challenge, the belt waits and Sunday is a normal daily brawl.

      How it works · sources · privacy
      Each round compares the two models on one benchmark, using the first benchmark in the round that both have a result for. Scores are each model's best reported result across reasoning settings. Damage is 2 × the gap in percentage points. If the gap is inside the combined margin of error (95%), the round is a tie and the crossfire grazes both fighters for 3 HP (never a knockout). In the price round the cheaper model wins: every halving of price is worth a 10-point win. A fight ends on a K.O., or by decision after the last round: the most HP wins, unanimously if it also won more rounds, split if not. Each lab has a signature move that plays on its model's biggest hit; it's just for fun and never changes the damage. Benchmarks measure narrow skills; treat this as a fun way to read them, not a verdict.
