How it works. Each round compares the two models on one benchmark, using the first benchmark in the round that both have a result for. Scores are each model's best reported result across reasoning settings. Damage is 2 × the gap in percentage points. If the gap is inside the combined margin of error (95%), the round is a tie. In the price round the cheaper model wins: every halving of price is worth a 10-point win. A fight ends on a K.O. or on HP after the last round. Benchmarks measure narrow skills; treat this as a fun way to read them, not a verdict.
